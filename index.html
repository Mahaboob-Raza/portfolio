RESEARCH

A Hybrid TF-IDF–SBERT Framework with Rule-Based Skill Verification for Explainable, Bias-Mitigated Resume Screening

This research presents a hybrid approach to resume screening that combines traditional keyword-based matching, semantic similarity, and deterministic skill verification to produce more explainable candidate rankings.

01 — TF-IDF

Keyword-based relevance and matching

15%
02 — SBERT

Semantic similarity and contextual understanding

45%
03 — Rule-Based Skill Verification

Explicit skill extraction and matching

40%

WHAT THE RESEARCH FOCUSES ON

The framework is designed to improve the explainability of automated resume screening while reducing the influence of personally identifying information during preprocessing.
